# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Simple Interest Calculator project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to creating an open and inclusive environment where everyone can contribute, ask questions, share ideas, report issues, and provide feedback respectfully.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being respectful of different opinions and experiences.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
* Showing empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
* Providing helpful and constructive suggestions.
* Respecting the work and contributions of others.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
* Publishing another person's private information without permission.
* Using inappropriate or offensive language.
* Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or open-source community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, commits, code, or other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the Simple Interest Calculator project in public spaces or project-related activities.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer through the communication channels available in the GitHub repository.

All reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and appropriately. Project maintainers will make reasonable efforts to respect the privacy and security of anyone reporting an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may follow these guidelines when addressing violations:

### 1. Correction

A private or public clarification may be provided when the behavior is considered inappropriate but does not cause significant harm.

### 2. Warning

A formal warning may be issued for repeated or more serious violations. Continued inappropriate behavior may result in further action.

### 3. Temporary Restrictions

Temporary restrictions from participating in project discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project activities may be applied when necessary.

### 4. Permanent Ban

A permanent ban from participating in the project may be applied when there is a repeated pattern of unacceptable behavior or a serious violation of this Code of Conduct.
